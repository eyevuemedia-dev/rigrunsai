Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026. RigRunsAI collects almost nothing: no accounts, no forms and no cookies of its own. The calculator runs in your browser, and the GPU, RAM and Mac details you enter are not sent to us.

The short version

  • No accounts, logins, comment boxes or sign-up forms. RigRunsAI does not ask you for personal information.
  • Page views are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not track you across sites.
  • RigRunsAI sets no cookies of its own and runs no ad networks or tracking pixels.
  • Affiliate links, such as Amazon, can set cookies on the retailer's site after you click through.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data.

Analytics

RigRunsAI uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. Cloudflare's beacon does not use cookies or local storage and does not fingerprint visitors. We see aggregated numbers only, such as which pages were viewed, referring sites, country, and browser or device type. We cannot identify you from them. Cloudflare processes this data under the Cloudflare privacy policy. A content blocker that blocks static.cloudflareinsights.com stops this counting, and every page still works.

Hosting

The site is static HTML hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub logs the IP address of visitors for security purposes, under the GitHub privacy statement. RigRunsAI does not receive or use those logs.

Affiliate links and cookies

The green buttons labeled "Check price on Amazon" are affiliate links marked rel="sponsored". As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. When you click one, Amazon may set a cookie in your browser so the purchase can be credited to us. That happens on Amazon's site, under the Amazon privacy notice. If we join other retailer or manufacturer affiliate programs, their links work the same way and are governed by that retailer's privacy policy. They will be named on the affiliate disclosure page. We get commission reports from those programs, not your name, address or what else you bought.

Email

If you email eyevueventures@gmail.com, we use your address and message only to reply, and we do not add you to any list. Ask and we will delete the thread.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data, or share it for advertising.
  • We do not run third-party ad networks, social media pixels or session recording.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The site is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. If we ever add a newsletter or any other form, this page will say what it collects before the form goes live.

Contact

RigRunsAI is published by EYEVUE Ventures. Privacy questions: eyevueventures@gmail.com.
