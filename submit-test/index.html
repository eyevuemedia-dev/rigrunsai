Submit your local AI benchmark

Ran a model on your own GPU, handheld or Mac? Send the tok/s and the proof. RigRunsAI's speed ranges are bandwidth-based estimates calibrated on published llama.cpp results. Your runs tell us where the estimates are high or low, especially for MoE models and partial offload.

What counts as proof

  • A screenshot of llama-bench output, ollama run --verbose (the eval rate line), or LM Studio's tok/s readout.
  • Make sure the model name and quant are visible, or list them in the form.
  • Or a link to a GitHub issue, Reddit post or gist with the full log.

What happens next

  1. Your entry lands in a private review queue. Nothing is published automatically.
  2. We check the photo or link against your numbers, the product's published specs and our own estimate. Results that don't add up get an email question (if you left one) or are set aside.
  3. Verified results appear on the matching page as a Verified owner test, credited to the name or handle you gave, or "a reader" if you left it blank. Photos are only published after review, with personal details cropped or blurred.

Tips for a clean measurement

  • Report generation (decode) speed in the main field and prompt processing separately. They differ by 10–50×.
  • State the context you actually ran. KV cache size changes both fit and speed.
  • If layers spilled to system RAM, pick "Partial CPU offload". It is the biggest swing factor we see.
