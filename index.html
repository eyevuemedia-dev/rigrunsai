FAQ

What is RigRunsAI?

A static fit guide for open local models on the GPU, handheld, or Mac you already have. Each answer is weights plus KV cache plus overhead, from published files, not a vibe check.

Does the calculator work without JavaScript?

The verdict, tables, and FAQs are HTML. The manual VRAM, RAM, and Mac form needs JavaScript. Without it, open a GPU or Mac page and read the model table.

Are these affiliate links?

No. There are no shopping links yet. The affiliate disclosure page says so.

Can I reuse the data?

Yes. /data/models.json and /data/hardware.json are CC-BY 4.0. Cite RigRunsAI and keep the source URLs with the specs.
