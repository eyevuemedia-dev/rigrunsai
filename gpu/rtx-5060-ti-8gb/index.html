RTX 5060 Ti 8GB for local AI: what fits, real speeds, and whether to pay $270 more for 16GB

Short version: 8 GB runs 7B–9B models as fast as the 16GB card, because it is the same chip with the same 448 GB/s. It does not hold gpt-oss-20b or 14B models. This page covers measured speeds on the same chip, how much context 8 GB really holds, and today's prices, which have moved the 8GB-vs-16GB gap from $50 to $270. Updated 2026-10-31.

Short answer

  • Fast on 7B–9B models: same GB206 chip and 448 GB/s as the 16GB card. On that card Qwen3 8B measured 69.2 tok/s at 4K (Hardware Corner) and Llama 3.1 8B 83.2 tok/s in Ollama (ComputingForGeeks).
  • 8 GB is the ceiling: gpt-oss-20b, every 14B model, Mistral Nemo and Gemma 3 12B spill to system RAM on our math. Gemma 4 12B is a tight fit.
  • Prices moved: launch was $379 vs $429 for the 16GB. On 2026-10-31 the cheapest new cards were $529.99 and $799.99 at Newegg (ComponentPrices), a $270 gap. Memory shortages are pushing GPU prices up (Tom's Hardware).
  • Verdict: buy the 8GB only if 9B-class chat and coding models are the job, or a used one near the $460 eBay median (GPU Sold). If you want 14B–20B models, the 16GB is still the card, even at $270 more.

Specs that matter for AI

NVIDIA sells the 5060 Ti with 8 GB or 16 GB of GDDR7 and otherwise identical specs. Token generation on a single card is limited by memory bandwidth, so the two run models that fit at the same speed. The only thing the 8GB card gives up is room.

The llama.cpp result lands inside our range. The Ollama short-prompt runs came in 6% to 14% above our high end, which assumes 80% of the bandwidth roofline. Llama 3.1 8B at 83.2 tok/s is about 91% of this card's theoretical ceiling for a 4.9 GB file, with an almost empty KV cache. Treat our upper number as conservative for short chats and the lower one as realistic once a long conversation fills the context.

Prompt processing is where the 4,608 CUDA cores show up: Hardware Corner measured Qwen3 8B prefill at 2,965 tok/s at 4K and 1,448 tok/s at 16K, so a 4,000-token paste is read in under two seconds.

How much context fits in 8 GB

The model file is only part of the bill. Every token of context adds KV cache, and on an 8 GB card that is usually what runs out first. ComputingForGeeks logged how much memory Ollama loaded for each model at its 4K default and at 64K context. Solid bar is 4K, faded bar is 64K, and the orange line marks 8 GB.

Data: ComputingForGeeks, Ollama 0.40.6 on an RTX 5060 Ti 16GB, memory as reported by ollama ps. Scale 0–14 GB.

  • Qwen3.5 9B barely grows with context, so it is the best long-context model for this card. 64K sits right at the limit, 32K is comfortable.
  • Llama 3.1 8B grows fast. Plan on about 16K with the default cache, more with a q8_0 cache (see Setup).
  • Gemma 4 12B already needs about 7.7 GB at 4K. It runs, but a desktop on the same card can push it over. Use it on a headless box or with integrated graphics driving the monitor.
  • gpt-oss-20b needs about 12.7 GB, so part of it always lives in system RAM on this card.

Setup notes for 8 GB

  • Software: Blackwell (compute capability 12.0) needs recent builds. Current Ollama, LM Studio and llama.cpp CUDA builds support it.
  • Shrink the KV cache: in Ollama set OLLAMA_FLASH_ATTENTION=1 and OLLAMA_KV_CACHE_TYPE=q8_0, which roughly halves cache memory (ComputingForGeeks). In llama.cpp use -fa on -ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0.
  • Set context on purpose: num_ctx in Ollama or -c in llama.cpp. Ask for 64K on Llama 3.1 8B and Ollama will quietly push layers to the CPU.
  • Free the VRAM: if your CPU has integrated graphics, plug the monitor into the motherboard so the desktop does not take a slice of the 8 GB.
  • MoE bigger than 8 GB: --cpu-moe or --n-cpu-moe N keeps expert weights in system RAM (llama.cpp PR #15077). Fast DDR5 and a PCIe 4.0 or 5.0 slot help, because the card has eight lanes.

Who should buy it

Buy the 8GB if your local AI is chat, summarizing and coding help with 7B to 9B models, if the card also has to game at 1080p, and if $270 matters to your budget. It runs those models as fast as the 16GB. A used one near $460 is better still. Buy the 16GB instead if gpt-oss-20b, Qwen3 14B or long-context work is the point. Those spill into system RAM on 8 GB and run about three to four times slower. Wait if you can: both cards cost 40% to 86% more than at launch, and prices that rose with the memory shortage can fall back.

FAQ

Is the RTX 5060 Ti 8GB good for local LLMs?

For 7B to 9B models, yes. It has the same chip and the same 448 GB/s as the 16GB card, so models that fit run at the same speed. On the 16GB version Hardware Corner measured Qwen3 8B at 69.2 tok/s at 4K context, and ComputingForGeeks measured Qwen3.5 9B at 67.7 and Llama 3.1 8B at 83.2 tok/s in Ollama. The limit is capacity: gpt-oss-20b, every 14B model, Mistral Nemo and Gemma 3 12B spill into system RAM on our math at 8K context.

RTX 5060 Ti 8GB or 16GB for AI?

At launch the answer was easy: the 16GB cost $50 more ($429 vs $379). On 2026-10-31 the cheapest new cards on Newegg were $529.99 for the 8GB and $799.99 for the 16GB, a $270 gap, according to ComponentPrices. Buy the 16GB if you want gpt-oss-20b or 14B models in VRAM, which run about three to four times faster than offloaded. Buy the 8GB only if 9B-class models cover your work, or if the price gap is what decides it.

How much context can 8 GB hold?

It depends on the model. In ComputingForGeeks' Ollama tests, Llama 3.1 8B grew from 5.3 GB loaded at 4K context to 13.4 GB at 64K, so on an 8 GB card it tops out around 16K with the default cache. Qwen3.5 9B grew only from 5.6 to 7.7 GB at 64K, so long context is practical. Setting the KV cache to q8_0 roughly halves its size.

What is the largest model that runs on an RTX 5060 Ti 8GB?

Fully in VRAM, about 9B at Q4_K_M with room for context, or Gemma 4 12B as a tight fit at 8K. Mixture-of-experts models such as Qwen3 30B-A3B and gpt-oss-20b run with part of the model in system RAM at roughly 18 to 34 tok/s on our estimate. Dense 14B to 32B models drop to single digits.

RTX 5060 Ti 8GB or RTX 5060 for AI?

Both have 8 GB of GDDR7 on a 128-bit bus, so the same models fit and token generation is bandwidth-bound on both. The 5060 Ti has 4,608 CUDA cores against 3,840, which helps prompt processing. On 2026-10-31 ComponentPrices listed the RTX 5060 from $459.99 new, $70 less than the 5060 Ti 8GB.

Is a used RTX 5060 Ti 8GB a good deal?

GPU Sold tracked a $460 median across 32 eBay sales in the 14 days to 2026-10-29, against $529.99 new. That is a fair price for a 9B-class chat card, but it is still close to what 12 GB and 16 GB cards cost used. Check our RTX 3060 12GB and RTX 5060 Ti 16GB pages before you buy.

What power supply does the RTX 5060 Ti need?

NVIDIA lists 180 W total graphics power, a 600 W system power supply (based on a Ryzen 9 9950X PC) and one 8-pin PCIe connector, or a 300 W PCIe Gen 5 cable.

What does the verdict for GeForce RTX 5060 Ti 8GB mean?

Memory is the GGUF file, plus KV cache at 8,192 tokens, plus a 0.5 GiB engine allowance, then 5% headroom. Fits means that total is at most 85% of fast memory. Tight means it still fits. Offload means it needs system RAM. No means it does not fit in VRAM plus system RAM after an 8 GB Windows reserve. The formula is on the methodology page.

Why is speed a range?

Decode speed is memory bandwidth divided by the bytes read per token. The high end is 80% of that roofline on the weights alone. The low end is 55% once the KV cache at the working context is included. Mixture-of-experts ranges add a fixed per-token overhead fitted to measured results (since 2026-10-26). Short-prompt Ollama runs on this chip came in up to 14% above our high end, so treat the ceiling as conservative for short chats.
