RTX 5070 for local AI: measured speeds, the 12 GB ceiling, and whether it is worth $900

Short version: the RTX 5070 runs every model up to 14B fast, with measured results inside our estimates. It cannot hold the 20B-class models that are now the sweet spot, and at today's prices it costs more than a 16 GB RTX 5060 Ti. Updated 2026-11-12.

Short answer

  • Fast inside 12 GB. Hardware Corner measured 85.8 tok/s on Qwen3 8B and 54.2 tok/s on Qwen3 14B at 4K, falling to 40.6 tok/s for 14B at 16K. Our estimates sat around those results.
  • 12 GB is the ceiling. 14B models are Tight at 8K. gpt-oss-20b, 24B and 27B models spill into system RAM. Hardware Corner lists no 32K result for Qwen3 14B on this card.
  • Bad price right now. $899.99 new at Newegg on 2026-11-12 (ComponentPrices), 64% over the $549 launch price; $800 used (GPU Sold). That is more than a new RTX 5060 Ti 16GB ($799.99).
  • Who should buy it: someone who games at 1440p first and runs 8B to 14B models second. For an AI-first build, take a 16 GB card. On a tight budget, take a used RTX 3060 12GB for the same models at about half the speed.

Read it this way: anything above about 10 tok/s reads faster than you do, so the 5070 is comfortable on every model that fits. The practical line: 12 GB holds every good model up to 14B, and nothing comfortable above it. The newest mid-size open models cluster at 20B to 32B, and most are mixture-of-experts. On a 12 GB card those run half in system RAM. That makes 16 GB the more future-proof buy in late 2026, even on a slower card.

Alternatives, and when each one wins

  • RTX 5060 Ti 16GB: our default AI pick at this budget. It holds gpt-oss-20b fully in VRAM and has room for longer context on 14B models. It is about 20% slower than the 5070 on models that fit both.
  • Used RTX 3060 12GB: same models, about 55% of the speed, about 40% of the price. Best if you just want 14B models to work.
  • RTX 5070 Ti: 16 GB plus 896 GB/s, so it is fast on everything up to 20B. At $1,319.99 it only makes sense if you also game at 4K.
  • RX 9070 / 9070 XT: 16 GB for $749.99 to $949.99 new. Good on llama.cpp with Vulkan or ROCm, but expect more setup friction than CUDA with some tools.
  • Used RTX 3090: the only card here that holds 32B dense models. At $1,618 it costs nearly twice the 5070.
  • RTX 4070 12GB: Hardware Corner suggests it as a cheaper 12 GB card. GPU Sold had too few recent sales to give a used median, so we cannot price it today.

Setup notes for 12 GB Blackwell

  • Update the stack: Blackwell needs CUDA 12.8-era builds. A current Ollama, LM Studio or llama.cpp release is fine; an old install may silently fall back to CPU. Check ollama ps shows 100% GPU.
  • Shrink the KV cache: OLLAMA_FLASH_ATTENTION=1 and OLLAMA_KV_CACHE_TYPE=q8_0 in Ollama, or -fa on -ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0 in llama.cpp. This is how a 14B model gets past 16K on 12 GB.
  • gpt-oss-20b: start with --n-cpu-moe 4 in llama.cpp and lower it until VRAM is nearly full (PR #15077). Fast DDR5 system RAM helps.
  • Free the desktop VRAM: on a CPU with integrated graphics, plug the monitor into the motherboard so Windows doesn't take a slice of the 12 GB.
  • Power: use a real 650 W unit. Use the included adapter with two separate 8-pin cables, not one daisy-chained cable.

Who should buy it

Buy the RTX 5070 if it is mainly a 1440p gaming card and local AI means 8B to 14B chat, coding and summarizing models. In that job it is fast, draws 250 W, and is fully supported. Look for one near or under $750 used, or wait for new prices to drift back toward the August low of $679.99.

Buy a 16 GB card instead if local AI is the reason you are upgrading. At $899.99, the 5070 costs more than an RTX 5060 Ti 16GB, which runs the 20B-class models the 5070 has to spill. Buy a used RTX 3060 12GB if the budget is under $400 and 14B models are enough.

FAQ

Is the RTX 5070 good for local LLMs?

For 7B to 14B models, yes. Hardware Corner measured 85.8 tok/s on Qwen3 8B and 54.2 tok/s on Qwen3 14B at 4K context. The catch is 12 GB: 20B-class models such as gpt-oss-20b do not fit fully, and at $899.99 new (2026-11-12) a 16 GB RTX 5060 Ti costs $100 less.

Can the RTX 5070 run gpt-oss-20b?

Not fully in VRAM. The MXFP4 file is about 11.3 GiB and needs about 12.6 GiB with an 8K cache, more than the 12 GB card can give a model. It runs with a few expert layers in system RAM using llama.cpp's --n-cpu-moe, but we have no measured RTX 5070 result for that setup.

RTX 5070 or RTX 5060 Ti 16GB for AI?

The 5070 is faster on models that fit in 12 GB, measuring 40.6 vs 32.9 tok/s on Qwen3 14B at 16K. The 5060 Ti 16GB holds 20B-class models and longer context, and costs less: $799.99 new and about $720 used versus $899.99 and $800 for the 5070. For an AI-first build we would take the 16 GB card.

RTX 5070 or a used RTX 3060 12GB?

Same 12 GB, so the same models fit. The 5070 is about 1.8 times faster at 16K (40.6 vs 22.7 tok/s on Qwen3 14B), but a used 3060 sells for about $340. If speed on 8B to 14B models is worth roughly $460 to you, the 5070 earns it. If not, the 3060 runs the same models.

What is a fair price for a used RTX 5070?

GPU Sold tracked a $800 median across 134 eBay sales in the 14 days to 2026-11-10, with most between $757 and $877. Founders Edition cards averaged $881 over 30 days. Under about $750 is a good price; above about $800, a new card with a warranty or a 16 GB card makes more sense.

What power supply does the RTX 5070 need?

NVIDIA lists 250 W total graphics power and a 650 W system power supply, with either two 8-pin PCIe cables through the included adapter or a 300 W or higher PCIe Gen 5 cable.

Does the RTX 5070 work with Ollama and LM Studio?

Yes, with current releases. Blackwell cards need recent CUDA 12.8-era builds; if an older Ollama or llama.cpp install falls back to CPU, update it.
