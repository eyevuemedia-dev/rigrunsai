RTX 4060 Ti 16GB for local AI: measured speeds, what 16 GB runs, and whether it is still worth buying

The 4060 Ti 16GB is the cheapest-looking way into 16 GB of NVIDIA VRAM, held back by a 128-bit bus. This page covers how fast it really runs (independent llama.cpp tests checked against our estimates), what fits, how it compares with the 5060 Ti, 3060 12GB and its own 8GB twin, and the price where it makes sense. Updated 2026-10-28.

Short answer

  • 16 GB at entry-level speed: gpt-oss-20b measured 63.2 tok/s at 4K and 31.1 at 128K. Qwen3 14B measured 27.4 tok/s at 4K (Hardware Corner).
  • Bandwidth is the catch: 288 GB/s on a 128-bit bus. The 5060 Ti 16GB (448 GB/s) generated 43% to 50% faster at 16K in the same ranking.
  • Not the 8GB card: same speed, but Qwen3 14B and gpt-oss-20b only fit in VRAM on the 16GB version.
  • Price: $499 at launch on 2023-07-18 (NVIDIA, VideoCardz). Hardware Corner listed a $400 average market price on 2026-10-28, against $549 for the 5060 Ti 16GB.
  • Verdict: fine if you already own one or find it used well below the 5060 Ti. Otherwise buy the 5060 Ti 16GB.

Specs that matter for AI

NVIDIA sold the 4060 Ti in 8 GB and 16 GB versions with otherwise identical specs. For local LLMs the 16 GB is the only one worth considering, and the 128-bit bus is its weak point: 288 GB/s is less than the older RTX 3060 12GB.

Setup notes

  • Software: Ada Lovelace is well supported. Current Ollama, LM Studio and llama.cpp CUDA builds work without special flags.
  • Context costs VRAM and speed: set num_ctx in Ollama or -c in llama.cpp to what you need, and use flash attention (-fa on). On a 288 GB/s card, long context slows generation more than on faster cards.
  • MoE bigger than 16 GB: --cpu-moe and --n-cpu-moe N keep expert weights in system RAM (llama.cpp PR #15077). Fast DDR5, 64 GB of RAM and a PCIe 4.0 slot help, because the card has only eight lanes.
  • Power and fit: NVIDIA lists a 550 W PSU. Most partner cards are two-slot with one 8-pin plug. Check the length against your case.

Who should buy it

Keep or buy a 4060 Ti 16GB if you already own one, or if you find one used below about $370, and your models are 20B or smaller. It is quiet, efficient and holds gpt-oss-20b with its full context. Skip it at new-card prices: the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB fits the same models and runs them 43% to 50% faster. Avoid the 8GB version for AI. For 12 GB on a budget, a used RTX 3060 12GB is the value pick. For 27B to 32B dense models, look at a used RTX 3090. See also the best models for 16 GB.

FAQ

Is the RTX 4060 Ti 16GB good for local LLMs?

It works, and 16 GB is the reason to own one. Hardware Corner measured gpt-oss-20b at 63.2 tok/s at 4K context and still 31.1 tok/s at 128K, and Qwen3 14B at 27.4 tok/s at 4K. It is slow for a 16 GB card because its memory bandwidth is only 288 GB/s. At today's prices the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB is the better new buy. A used 4060 Ti 16GB makes sense only at a clear discount.

RTX 4060 Ti 16GB or RTX 5060 Ti 16GB for AI?

The 5060 Ti 16GB. Both have 16 GB, so the same models fit, but the 5060 Ti has 448 GB/s of GDDR7 against 288 GB/s. In Hardware Corner's 16K ranking it generated 43% to 50% faster: 51.4 vs 34.3 tok/s on Qwen3 8B, 32.9 vs 22.4 on Qwen3 14B and 82.4 vs 57.8 on gpt-oss-20b. Prompt processing is about the same.

RTX 4060 Ti 16GB or 8GB for local AI?

The 16GB. Speed is identical on models that fit both, because the bandwidth is the same 288 GB/s. The difference is capacity. On our math at 8K context, Qwen3 14B and gpt-oss-20b fit on the 16GB card but spill into system RAM on the 8GB card, where they drop to about 4 to 6 and 13 to 18 tok/s.

RTX 4060 Ti 16GB or used RTX 3060 12GB?

The 3060 12GB generated faster on the models both can hold in Hardware Corner's 16K ranking (42.0 vs 34.3 tok/s on Qwen3 8B, a tie on Qwen3 14B) because it has more bandwidth, 360 vs 288 GB/s, and it costs much less used. The 4060 Ti 16GB prefills prompts faster and holds 20B to 24B models that do not fit in 12 GB.

What is the largest model that fits on an RTX 4060 Ti 16GB?

On our math at 8K context, Mistral Small 3.2 24B and Devstral Small 2 24B at Q4_K_M need about 15.9 GiB, a tight fit, at roughly 10 to 16 tok/s. gpt-oss-20b at MXFP4 fits with room to spare. Dense 27B to 32B models need system RAM and fall to low single digits.

Does the PCIe x8 interface matter for AI?

Not when the whole model is in VRAM. The 4060 Ti uses eight PCIe 4.0 lanes, even on cards with a full-length connector. That only matters when you offload layers to system RAM, and it matters more in an older PCIe 3.0 board, where the link runs at half the PCIe 4.0 rate.

What power supply does an RTX 4060 Ti 16GB need?

NVIDIA lists 165 W total graphics power, about 140 W average in games, 7 W idle and a 550 W system power supply. Most partner cards use one 8-pin PCIe plug. There is no Founders Edition of the 16GB model, so size and plugs depend on the partner card.

Why is speed a range?

Decode speed is memory bandwidth divided by the bytes read per token. The high end is 80% of that roofline on the weights alone. The low end is 55% once the KV cache at the working context is included. Mixture-of-experts ranges add a fixed per-token overhead fitted to measured results (since 2026-10-26). On this card, measured Qwen3 8B and gpt-oss-20b speeds fall inside our ranges and Qwen3 14B at 4K is about 5% above our ceiling.
