Six years on, the 3090 is still the cheapest single card with 24 GB. This page covers what fits, how fast it really runs (independent llama.cpp tests checked against our estimates), how it stacks up against the 5070 Ti, 4090 and 5090, and what to check before buying one used. Updated 2026-10-25.

Short answer

  • Why people buy it: 24 GB at 936 GB/s. Dense 30B to 32B models fit fully in VRAM. Qwen3 32B measured 35.1 tok/s at 4K and Gemma 4 31B 31.4 tok/s at 32K (Hardware Corner).
  • Fast on mid-size models: Qwen3 14B measured 70.0 tok/s at 4K. gpt-oss-20b measured 147.5 tok/s at 4K and still 62.2 at 128K.
  • Long-context MoE: Gemma 4 26B-A4B ran to 256K context (64.4 tok/s). Qwen3.5 35B in MXFP4 ran at 128K (79.4 tok/s).
  • Price: listed at $1,000 average used market value on 2026-10-25, up from about $800 in January 2026 (Hardware Corner: current, January). That is roughly $42 per GB of VRAM.
  • Trade-offs: it launched in 2020, so original warranties have usually expired. It also draws 350 W and needs a 750 W PSU (NVIDIA).

Specs that matter for AI

The 3090 is a 2020 Ampere flagship. For local AI, the two numbers that still count are 24 GB of capacity and a 384-bit bus. Its compute is a generation behind, which mostly affects prompt processing, not reading speed.

What to run on it

Biggest dense model Qwen3 32B or Gemma 4 31B at Q4_K_M. Keep context at 8K to 16K for Qwen3 32B. Gemma 4 31B ran to 32K in testing.
ollama run gemma4:31b
Fast all-rounder Qwen3 30B-A3B at Q4_K_M: 153.6 tok/s at 4K and 87.2 at 32K, fully on the GPU.
ollama run qwen3:30b-a3b
Long documents gpt-oss-20b (MXFP4) at the full 128K context, or Gemma 4 26B-A4B, which ran at 256K.
llama-server -m model.gguf -ngl 99 -fa on -c 131072

Context costs VRAM. In Ollama set num_ctx, and in llama.cpp use -c. Hardware Corner ran its tests with flash attention on (-fa), which the 3090 supports.

Used RTX 3090 checklist

  • Confirm the card. Check that nvidia-smi or GPU-Z reports an RTX 3090 with 24 GB. A 3090 Ti also has 24 GB but is a different board drawing 450 W, and it needs an 850 W PSU (NVIDIA).
  • Load test it. Run a long llama.cpp or stress session and watch the memory junction temperature in HWiNFO, not just the GPU core temperature. The 3090 has GDDR6X modules on both sides of the board. Memory heat, pads and fans are what age on a four-to-six-year-old card.
  • Look before you pay. Check for fan wobble, a bent PCB, missing screws and marks from re-padding. Check the power plugs for heat discoloration.
  • Know the plugs and size. The Founders Edition is 313 mm, 3-slot, with a 12-pin adapter from 2× 8-pin. Many partner cards are longer and use 3× 8-pin. Use separate PSU cables, not one daisy-chained cable.
  • Prefer a return window. Buy refurbished or from a marketplace that offers returns, because original warranties from 2020 to 2022 have usually lapsed.

Setup notes

  • Runtime: llama.cpp with CUDA for GGUF. Ollama and LM Studio for convenience. vLLM or ExLlamaV2 for batched serving. Hardware Corner lists all four as common on the 3090.
  • Power cap: decode is bandwidth-bound, so capping the card with nvidia-smi -pl often costs less speed than it saves in heat. Measure the trade on your own card before relying on it.
  • Two cards = 48 GB. llama.cpp and vLLM split a model across cards over PCIe, and NVLink is optional. On our math Llama 3.3 70B at Q4_K_M needs about 44.7 GiB, which fits in 48 GB. Hardware Corner puts multi-3090 builds at 1,200 to 1,600 W PSUs.
  • MoE bigger than 24 GB: keep expert weights in system RAM with --cpu-moe or --n-cpu-moe N (llama.cpp PR #15077) to run gpt-oss-120b-class models on one card at reduced speed.

Who should buy it

Buy a used 3090 if 24 GB is the goal: 30B to 32B dense models at interactive speed, long-context MoE, or a path to a 48 GB two-card rig. Skip it if 20B and smaller models cover your work. A new RTX 5070 Ti or 5060 Ti 16GB runs those with a warranty and less heat. See also the best models for 24 GB.

FAQ

Is a used RTX 3090 still worth it for local LLMs in 2026?

Yes, if you need 24 GB. It is the cheapest way to hold 30B to 32B models fully in VRAM on one card. Hardware Corner measured Qwen3 32B at 35.1 tok/s at 4K context and Gemma 4 31B at 31.4 tok/s at 32K. Its average used market value was listed at $1,000 when we checked on 2026-10-25, up from about $800 in January 2026. If 20B and smaller models cover your work, a new 16 GB card is simpler.

What is the largest model that fits on an RTX 3090?

Dense models up to about 32B at Q4_K_M. On our math Qwen3 32B needs about 22.0 GiB with 8K of context, which is a tight fit. Hardware Corner ran Qwen3 32B to 16K context and Gemma 4 31B to 32K. Mixture-of-experts models go further. Qwen3.5 35B in MXFP4 ran at the full 128K context at 79.4 tok/s.

Can an RTX 3090 run 70B models?

Not fully in VRAM. Llama 3.3 70B at Q4_K_M needs about 44.7 GiB on our math. One card has to offload to system RAM, which drops to single-digit tok/s (Hardware Corner), and our estimate is about 1 tok/s. Two 3090s give 48 GB, which clears that 44.7 GiB on our math. We have no measured dual-3090 70B figure to quote.

RTX 3090 or RTX 5070 Ti for AI?

On models that fit in 16 GB they are about even. Hardware Corner's 16K ranking has 87.5 vs 87.5 tok/s on Qwen3 8B, 52.1 vs 58.0 on Qwen3 14B and 128.5 vs 133.1 on gpt-oss-20b. The 5070 Ti is new, has a warranty, draws 300 W instead of 350 W and processes prompts about 37% to 52% faster. Only the 3090 holds Qwen3 32B and Gemma 4 31B in VRAM. Pick on model size, not speed.

RTX 3090 or RTX 4090 for local LLMs?

The 4090 has the same 24 GB, so it fits the same models. It is about 20% to 33% faster at generation in Hardware Corner's 16K ranking, for example 69.1 vs 52.1 tok/s on Qwen3 14B, and about 2 to 2.6 times as fast at prompt processing. For a single user the 3090 delivers most of the experience at a fraction of the price.

What power supply does an RTX 3090 need?

NVIDIA lists 350 W graphics card power and a 750 W system power supply. The Founders Edition uses two 8-pin PCIe cables through the included 12-pin adapter. Many partner cards use three 8-pin plugs. Hardware Corner suggests 850 W for a single-card AI workstation and 1,200 to 1,600 W for multi-3090 builds.

What does the verdict for GeForce RTX 3090 mean?

Memory is the GGUF file, plus KV cache at 8,192 tokens, plus a 0.5 GiB engine allowance, then 5% headroom. Fits means that total is at most 85% of fast memory. Tight means it still fits. Offload means it needs system RAM. No means it does not fit in VRAM plus system RAM after an 8 GB Windows reserve. The formula is on the methodology page.

Why is speed a range?

Decode speed is memory bandwidth divided by the bytes read per token. The high end is 80% of that roofline on the weights alone. The low end is 55% once the KV cache at the working context is included. It is not a benchmark, which is why this page also lists measured results. On the 3090 our dense-model ranges hold up well. Our mixture-of-experts ranges used to run high, so since 2026-10-26 they include a per-token overhead fitted to measured results.
