Specs that matter for AI

The 5070 Ti is a Blackwell card with the same 16 GB and 256-bit bus as the 5080, slightly fewer cores and a lower price. For local LLMs the bandwidth is what you are buying: 896 GB/s is twice the 5060 Ti 16GB and within 4% of a 3090.

Setup notes

  • Driver and CUDA: Blackwell needs a recent driver and software built for CUDA 12.8 or newer. Older prebuilt wheels and containers may not include the new architecture (NVIDIA Blackwell compatibility guide). Current Ollama, LM Studio and llama.cpp builds are the low-friction route.
  • Context costs VRAM: with 16 GB, long context is the first thing to run out. Set num_ctx in Ollama or -c in llama.cpp, and use flash attention (-fa on).
  • MoE bigger than 16 GB: --cpu-moe and --n-cpu-moe N keep expert weights in system RAM (llama.cpp PR #15077). Fast DDR5 and 64 GB of RAM help.
  • Power and fit: NVIDIA lists a 750 W PSU. Use the native 16-pin PCIe Gen 5 cable if your PSU has one, seat it fully, and check the partner card's length and slot count against your case.

Who should buy it

Buy a 5070 Ti if your models are 20B or smaller, or 24B at short context, and you want speed, fast prompt processing and a warranty. It is the strongest 16 GB card for the money for LLM work, ahead of the 5080 on value. Skip it if you need 27B to 32B dense models in VRAM. A used RTX 3090 gives you 24 GB at similar speed. On a tight budget, the 5060 Ti 16GB fits the same models at about 60% of the speed. See also the best models for 16 GB.

FAQ

Is the RTX 5070 Ti good for local LLMs?

Yes, for models up to about 20B, or 24B at a tight fit. Hardware Corner measured gpt-oss-20b at 156.0 tok/s at 4K context and still 66.6 tok/s at 128K, and Qwen3 14B at 74.3 tok/s at 4K. Its limit is 16 GB of VRAM. Dense 27B to 32B models spill into system RAM and slow to single digits on our math.

What is the largest model that fits on an RTX 5070 Ti?

On our math at 8K context, Mistral Small 3.2 24B and Devstral Small 2 24B at Q4_K_M need about 15.9 GiB, a tight fit with little room for longer context. gpt-oss-20b at MXFP4 needs about 12.6 GiB and fits comfortably. Qwen3 32B needs about 22.0 GiB and does not fit.

RTX 5070 Ti or used RTX 3090 for AI?

On models that fit in 16 GB they are close: 87.5 vs 87.5 tok/s on Qwen3 8B, 58.0 vs 52.1 on Qwen3 14B and 133.1 vs 128.5 on gpt-oss-20b at 16K (Hardware Corner ranking). The 5070 Ti prefills prompts 37% to 52% faster, draws 300 W instead of 350 W and comes with a new-card warranty. The 3090 has 24 GB and holds 32B models in VRAM. Pick on model size.

RTX 5070 Ti or RTX 5080 for local AI?

Both have 16 GB, so the same models fit. In Hardware Corner's 16K ranking the 5080 generated 5% to 10% faster (94.1 vs 87.5 tok/s on Qwen3 8B, 64.0 vs 58.0 on Qwen3 14B) and prefilled up to 10% faster. For LLM work the extra money buys little.

How much faster is the 5070 Ti than the 5060 Ti 16GB?

About 61% to 76% faster at generation at 16K in Hardware Corner's ranking, for example 58.0 vs 32.9 tok/s on Qwen3 14B. Both have 16 GB, so they fit the same models. The 5070 Ti has twice the bandwidth (896 vs 448 GB/s).

What power supply does an RTX 5070 Ti need?

NVIDIA lists 300 W total graphics power and a 750 W system power supply. Power comes through a 300 W PCIe Gen 5 cable or two 8-pin PCIe cables with an adapter. There is no Founders Edition, so size and plugs depend on the partner card.

What does the verdict for GeForce RTX 5070 Ti mean?

Memory is the GGUF file, plus KV cache at 8,192 tokens, plus a 0.5 GiB engine allowance, then 5% headroom. Fits means that total is at most 85% of fast memory. Tight means it still fits. Offload means it needs system RAM. No means it does not fit in VRAM plus system RAM after an 8 GB Windows reserve. The formula is on the methodology page.

Why is speed a range?

Decode speed is memory bandwidth divided by the bytes read per token. The high end is 80% of that roofline on the weights alone. The low end is 55% once the KV cache at the working context is included. Since 2026-10-26 mixture-of-experts ranges add a fixed per-token overhead fitted to measured results. On the 5070 Ti, measured 4K and 16K speeds for Qwen3 8B, Qwen3 14B and gpt-oss-20b all fall inside our ranges.
