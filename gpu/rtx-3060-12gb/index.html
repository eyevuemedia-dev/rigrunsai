Specs that matter for AI

Token generation on one card is limited mostly by memory bandwidth, and what you can run at all is limited by memory size. The 3060 12GB has more of both than its gaming tier suggests, which is why it outlived several newer 8 GB cards for local AI.

Two things stand out. First, speed falls with context: Qwen3 8B loses about 42% going from 4K to 32K, because every new token reads the growing KV cache as well as the weights. Second, prompt processing is the 3060's weak spot. At 678 tok/s, a 16,000-token document takes about 24 seconds to read before the first word comes back on Qwen3 14B. A newer card cuts that wait a lot, so for long-document work that matters more than the generation number.

How much context fits in 12 GB

The model file is only part of the bill. Every token of context adds KV cache, and on 12 GB that is what runs out first on 14B models.

  • 8B–9B models: 32K is practical in VRAM (Qwen3 8B measured 31.9 tok/s at 32K). Newer models with small caches, such as Qwen3.5 9B, go further.
  • 12B models: Gemma 4 12B and Mistral Nemo 12B fit at 8K with headroom on our math (7.95 and 9.15 GiB). Plan on 16K to 32K depending on the model.
  • 14B models: about 16K. Qwen3 14B, Phi-4 and the DeepSeek-R1 14B distill are Tight at 8K on our math (10.6–11.0 GiB). Past about 16K, Ollama quietly pushes layers to the CPU.
  • gpt-oss-20b: loads at about 12.7 GB even at 64K on a 16 GB card (ComputingForGeeks), so on 12 GB a slice of it always sits in system RAM.

A q8_0 KV cache roughly halves cache memory and is the cheapest way to buy back context (see Setup).

Alternatives worth checking

  • RTX 5060 Ti 16GB: 4 GB more room for gpt-oss-20b and 14B with long context, and about 46% faster generation. $720 used or $809.99 new, so it costs about twice a used 3060.
  • RTX 4060 Ti 16GB: 16 GB but only 288 GB/s, so it generates no faster than the 3060. Worth it only for the extra memory, and at a $660 used median it is rarely worth it.
  • Intel Arc B580 12GB: also 12 GB, $362 used. It runs llama.cpp through SYCL or Vulkan rather than CUDA, so check support for the tools you use first.
  • RTX 5070: the same 12 GB at about 1.8× the generation speed, but $909.99 new. Same models fit, so it only buys speed. See the 3060 12GB to 5070 upgrade page.
  • Used RTX 3090: 24 GB and 936 GB/s for 24B to 32B models in VRAM, at about 2.3× the 3060's speed. It now costs about five used 3060s.
  • RTX 5060 Ti 8GB or RTX 4060: newer, but 8 GB. For local AI the old 3060 12GB holds more.

Two 3060s for 24 GB?

Because used 3060s are cheap and the 3090 is not, two 3060 12GB cards are the cheapest way to 24 GB of CUDA memory: about $680 against a $1,618 used 3090 median on 2026-11-10. Ollama and llama.cpp split a model's layers across both cards automatically, so a 32B model at Q4 that cannot run on one 3060 fits on two.

The catch is speed. With a layer split the cards take turns: each token still reads every weight once, at one card's 360 GB/s. Qwen3 32B at Q4_K_M is about 19.8 GB of weights, so the bandwidth ceiling is about 18 tok/s and our usual 55–80% range gives roughly 10 to 14 tok/s. That is our estimate, not a measurement. Hardware Corner measured 35.1 tok/s for the same model on one 3090 (source).

Setup notes for 12 GB

  • Software: Ampere (compute capability 8.6) is supported by every current CUDA build of Ollama, LM Studio and llama.cpp.
  • Shrink the KV cache: in Ollama set OLLAMA_FLASH_ATTENTION=1 and OLLAMA_KV_CACHE_TYPE=q8_0 (ComputingForGeeks). In llama.cpp use -fa on -ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0. This is what gets a 14B model past 16K.
  • Set context on purpose: num_ctx in Ollama or -c in llama.cpp. Check ollama ps: anything other than 100% GPU means part of the model spilled.
  • Free the VRAM: if your CPU has integrated graphics, plug the monitor into the motherboard so the desktop does not use part of the 12 GB.
  • gpt-oss-20b and other MoE models: --n-cpu-moe N keeps a few expert layers in system RAM and the rest on the card (llama.cpp PR #15077). The full x16 PCIe 4.0 link helps here.
  • Two cards: llama.cpp splits by layer by default. Use --tensor-split 1,1 to balance two identical cards.

Who should buy it

Buy a used 3060 12GB if your budget is under about $400, your models are 8B to 14B, and you want CUDA so every tool works on the first try. Near $340 it is still the best-value card on this site for that job. Do not buy it new at $459.99. For about $350 more a new RTX 5060 Ti 16GB holds 20B models and runs about 46% faster, and a used 5060 Ti 16GB is $720. Skip it if you need 24B–32B models to run quickly. Two 3060s fit them slowly, and a 3090 runs them well.

FAQ

Is the RTX 3060 12GB still good for local LLMs in 2026?

Yes, for 7B to 14B models. Hardware Corner measured Qwen3 8B at 55.2 tok/s at 4K context and Qwen3 14B at 31.2 tok/s at 4K and 22.7 tok/s at 16K, all in VRAM with llama.cpp. That is faster than you can read. The limits are 12 GB of memory and 360 GB/s of bandwidth: 20B and larger models spill into system RAM, and a newer card such as the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB generates about 46% faster in the same ranking.

What is the largest model an RTX 3060 12GB can run?

Fully in VRAM, a 14B model at Q4_K_M with about 16K of context. Hardware Corner's largest in-VRAM result was Qwen3 14B at 16K (22.7 tok/s). gpt-oss-20b needs about 12.7 GB loaded, so a little of it lives in system RAM. Dense 24B to 32B models drop to low single digits on one card.

Should I buy the RTX 3060 12GB new or used?

Used. On 2026-11-10 the cheapest new 3060 12GB at Newegg was $459.99 (ComponentPrices), while GPU Sold tracked a $340 median across 296 US eBay sales in 14 days. At $459.99 new you are about $350 from a new RTX 5060 Ti 16GB, which holds 20B models and generates roughly 46% faster. At $340 used, the 3060 is still the cheapest 12 GB CUDA card.

How do I avoid buying the RTX 3060 8GB by mistake?

Check the memory size and bus in the listing. NVIDIA sells the RTX 3060 as 12 GB on a 192-bit bus or 8 GB on a 128-bit bus. The 8GB version has a third less bandwidth (about 240 GB/s against 360) and a third less memory, which pushes 12B and 14B models off the card. Used 8GB cards sold for a $257 median on eBay, so they can look like a bargain.

Is a dual RTX 3060 setup worth it for 24 GB?

It is the cheapest way to 24 GB of CUDA memory: two used cards at about $340 each cost about $680, against a $1,618 used RTX 3090 median on 2026-11-10. llama.cpp and Ollama split the layers across both cards, so a 32B model fits. Speed does not add up, though. With a layer split each token still reads the whole model at one card's 360 GB/s, so our estimate for Qwen3 32B is about 10 to 14 tok/s, against 35.1 tok/s measured on a 3090. You need two spaced PCIe slots and Hardware Corner suggests an 850 to 1,000 W power supply.

RTX 3060 12GB or RTX 4060 Ti 16GB for AI?

The 3060 generates about as fast (Hardware Corner puts the 4060 Ti 16GB at 99% of the 3060 in its token-generation ranking, because the 4060 Ti has only 288 GB/s). The 4060 Ti's extra 4 GB holds gpt-oss-20b and 14B models with long context. Used 4060 Ti 16GB cards sold for a $660 median, almost twice the 3060.

What power supply does the RTX 3060 12GB need?

NVIDIA lists 170 W graphics card power and a 550 W system power supply, based on a Core i9-10900K PC. Hardware Corner recommends a quality 650 W unit for a single card and 850 to 1,000 W for two.

What does the verdict for GeForce RTX 3060 12GB mean?

Memory is the GGUF file, plus KV cache at 8,192 tokens, plus a 0.5 GiB engine allowance, then 5% headroom. Fits means that total is at most 85% of fast memory. Tight means it still fits. Offload means it needs system RAM. No means it does not fit in VRAM plus system RAM after an 8 GB Windows reserve. The formula is on the methodology page.

Why is speed a range?

Decode speed is memory bandwidth divided by the bytes read per token. The high end is 80% of that roofline on the weights alone. The low end is 55% once the KV cache at the working context is included. Mixture-of-experts ranges add a fixed per-token overhead fitted to measured results (since 2026-10-26). Hardware Corner's measured 3060 results land inside our ranges.
