Specs that matter for AI

Both 5060 Ti versions use the same GB206 chip and the same 448 GB/s memory system. Only capacity differs, which is why speed on small models is identical and the 16GB pulls ahead the moment a model or its context passes 8 GB.

What to run on it

Daily driver gpt-oss-20b (MXFP4). Fits with the full 128K context, according to Hardware Corner's test. Fast enough for chat and coding.
ollama run gpt-oss:20b
Best 14B Qwen3 14B at Q4_K_M, about 9 GB. Keep context at 16K to 32K. Hardware Corner puts the practical ceiling near 45K on this card.
ollama run qwen3:14b
Bigger brains via MoE Qwen3 30B-A3B or Gemma 4 26B-A4B with experts in system RAM. Only a few billion parameters are active per token, so it stays usable.
llama-server -m model.gguf -ngl 99 --cpu-moe -c 16384

--cpu-moe keeps all expert weights in system RAM. --n-cpu-moe N moves only the first N layers' experts, so you can fill the rest of the 16 GB (llama.cpp PR #15077). One owner test ran a 30B MoE this way using about 2.4 GiB of VRAM at 23 to 26 tok/s (c42). Leaving more experts on the GPU should be faster.

Setup notes from real deployments

  • Set the context on purpose. The KV cache grows with context and shares the 16 GB with the weights. In Ollama set num_ctx. In llama.cpp use -c. Qwen3 14B tops out near 45K on this card.
  • Pick the runtime for the job. llama.cpp or LM Studio for one fast user. Ollama for convenience, about 8% to 13% slower in the tests above. vLLM for several users at once: 65 tok/s single-stream, but about 385 tok/s total at 16 streams.
  • Watch airflow on long jobs. In a sustained 180 W soak inside a server chassis on default fan curves, the card hit 81 °C with hardware thermal slowdown. More slot airflow held it at 73 °C (c42). In a desktop, give it a clear intake path if you run batch jobs overnight.
  • Old board? Still fine. PCIe 5.0 x8 drops to 4.0 x8 or 3.0 x8 on older boards. That matters for model load time and RAM offload, not for a model that sits fully in VRAM.
  • Two cards = 32 GB. Hardware Corner reports a dual 5060 Ti 16GB setup reaching Qwen3 30B at about 147K context and Qwen3 32B at about 45K, the cheapest route to the 30B class without workstation cards (source). You need two x8-capable slots and room for two 180 W cards.

Who should buy it

Buy it if you want a new, warrantied, efficient card for 8B to 20B models, gpt-oss-20b with long context, or a first step toward a two-card 32 GB rig. Skip it if your goal is 24B to 32B dense models at interactive speed. A used 24 GB RTX 3090 or a 32 GB card is the real answer there, and our 24 GB and 32 GB pages show what they unlock.

FAQ

Is 16 GB enough for local LLMs?

For one person, yes, for models up to about 20B. gpt-oss-20b (MXFP4) runs fully in VRAM and Hardware Corner measured it at 92.1 tok/s at 4K context and still 43.8 tok/s at 128K. Every 14B model at Q4_K_M fits with room for 16K to 32K of context. 24B models at Q4_K_M are a tight fit at 8K. Dense 27B to 32B models need system RAM offload and drop to roughly 4 to 16 tok/s on our estimate.

RTX 5060 Ti 16GB or 8GB for AI?

Buy the 16GB if 14B to 20B models are the goal. NVIDIA's launch prices were $429 and $379, a $50 gap, but on 2026-10-31 the cheapest new cards at Newegg were $799.99 and $529.99, a $270 gap (ComponentPrices). On our fit math the extra 8 GB moves gpt-oss-20b and every 14B model in the catalog from system RAM offload into VRAM. Qwen3 14B goes from about 8 to 12 tok/s to about 24 to 40 tok/s. Models of 9B and smaller run at the same speed on both cards because both have 448 GB/s.

Is the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB faster than the RTX 4060 Ti 16GB for LLMs?

Yes, by roughly 45%. Both have 16 GB on a 128-bit bus, but the 5060 Ti uses 28 Gbps GDDR7 for 448 GB/s against 288 GB/s. In Hardware Corner's 16K-context ranking it generated 51.4 vs 34.3 tok/s on Qwen3 8B, 32.9 vs 22.4 on Qwen3 14B and 82.4 vs 57.8 on gpt-oss-20b.

RTX 5060 Ti 16GB or a used RTX 3090?

A used RTX 3090 has 24 GB and 936 GB/s, so it is faster (52.1 vs 32.9 tok/s on Qwen3 14B at 16K in Hardware Corner's ranking) and holds 30B-class models in VRAM. Hardware Corner put used 3090s around $800 in January 2026, against $429 at launch for the 5060 Ti 16GB (about $800 new on 2026-10-31), and the 3090 draws more power. Buy the 3090 if you need 24B to 32B models at speed. Buy the 5060 Ti if 20B and below covers your work.

Can the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB run 30B models?

Not fully in VRAM at Q4. Mixture-of-experts models such as Qwen3 30B-A3B run well with their experts in system RAM: one owner test kept a 30B MoE's experts in host RAM, used about 2.4 GiB of VRAM and decoded at 23 to 26 tok/s. Dense 30B to 32B models at Q4_K_M need about 22 GiB, so they spill to RAM and slow to about 4 to 5 tok/s on our estimate.

What power supply does the RTX 5060 Ti need?

NVIDIA lists 180 W total graphics power, a 600 W system power supply (based on a Ryzen 9 9950X PC) and one 8-pin PCIe connector, or a 300 W PCIe Gen 5 cable.

What does the verdict for GeForce RTX 5060 Ti 16GB mean?

Memory is the GGUF file, plus KV cache at 8,192 tokens, plus a 0.5 GiB engine allowance, then 5% headroom. Fits means that total is at most 85% of fast memory. Tight means it still fits. Offload means it needs system RAM. No means it does not fit in VRAM plus system RAM after an 8 GB Windows reserve. The formula is on the methodology page.

Why is speed a range?

Decode speed is memory bandwidth divided by the bytes read per token. The high end is 80% of that roofline on the weights alone. The low end is 55% once the KV cache at the working context is included. It is not a benchmark, which is why this page also lists measured results.
