GPU upgrades for local AI: what a new card actually unlocks

An upgrade helps local AI in one of two ways: more memory lets a bigger model fit, or more bandwidth makes the models you already run faster. Each comparison below runs our full catalog on both cards at 8K context so you can see which it is.

UpgradeWhat changes
GeForce RTX 3060 12GB to GeForce RTX 5070Same 12 GB either way. GeForce RTX 3060 12GB to GeForce RTX 5070 does not unlock a larger catalog model at 8K, it raises bandwidth. Mistral Nemo 12B Instruct moves from 22–39 tok/s to 42–72 tok/s.
GeForce RTX 4070 Ti Super to GeForce RTX 5080Same 16 GB either way. GeForce RTX 4070 Ti Super to GeForce RTX 5080 does not unlock a larger catalog model at 8K, it raises bandwidth. Qwen2.5 14B Instruct moves from 35–60 tok/s to 50–85 tok/s.
GeForce RTX 4070 to GeForce RTX 5070Same 12 GB either way. GeForce RTX 4070 to GeForce RTX 5070 does not unlock a larger catalog model at 8K, it raises bandwidth. Mistral Nemo 12B Instruct moves from 31–54 tok/s to 42–72 tok/s.
GeForce RTX 4090 to GeForce RTX 5090GeForce RTX 5090 newly fits Qwen2.5 32B Instruct, Qwen3 32B, and Qwen3.6 35B-A3B at the recommended quant and 8K context, which GeForce RTX 4090 does not.
Radeon RX 7800 XT to Radeon RX 9070 XTSame 16 GB either way. Radeon RX 7800 XT to Radeon RX 9070 XT does not unlock a larger catalog model at 8K, it raises bandwidth. Qwen2.5 14B Instruct moves from 32–56 tok/s to 33–57 tok/s.

FAQ

Is more VRAM or more bandwidth the better upgrade?

Buy VRAM when a model you want is in Offload or No on your current card. Buy bandwidth when the model already fits and you want more tokens per second.
