FAQ

What does best mean?

The model that fits, with the most active parameters. Dense models count every parameter. MoE models count the experts that actually run, plus attention and embeddings. It is not a quality benchmark.

Which PC is this?

12 GB of VRAM at RTX 4070-class, 504 GB/s, plus 32 GB of system RAM. A card with the same VRAM and less bandwidth fits the same models and runs them slower.

Why might a tighter model look smarter?

Tight still loads. It is kept out of the pick because an 8K chat can tip it into swapping. The table shows those models under the pick.
